PROJECT 01 · B.E. ACADEMIC PROJECT

Smart Energy Meter with Remote Monitoring and Control

ESP32-based IoT energy meter designed to measure AC voltage and current, calculate energy use, and support remote monitoring and load control.

ESP32PT / CTLM358Blynk IoTWi-Fi
  • Used PT and CT inputs with LM358 precision rectifier circuits to measure AC voltage and current.
  • Processed measurements and calculated active power and energy consumption (kWh).
  • Displayed readings on a 16×2 LCD and enabled real-time monitoring through Blynk.
  • Integrated relay-based smartphone load switching and threshold-based high-consumption alerts.
PROJECT 02 · DIPLOMA PROJECT · JAN–APR 2022

Collision Avoidance System for Hairpin Curves

A blind-curve warning prototype that detects approaching vehicles from both directions and provides visual and audible alerts.

NodeMCU ESP8266HC-SR0416×2 LCDLEDsBuzzer
  • Built the prototype using two HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensors.
  • Implemented distance-based detection for vehicles approaching from either direction.
  • Used red LEDs, a buzzer, and an LCD to communicate warnings before vehicles enter the bend.
